# Clinical review gate

## Required sections (use these exact headings)
1. **Findings** — numbered issues, each tagged critical / major / minor, with the exact location.
2. **Safety** — anything that could harm a patient if followed (wrong dose, missed contraindication, missing red flag, unsafe discharge advice).
3. **Evidence** — quality of the sources (GRADE-style: high / moderate / low / very low), recency, applicability to the patient and setting; flag claims without a source.
4. **Verdict** — one of: *ready*, *ready with edits* (list them), *not ready* (why).

## Rules
- Review the text that exists; do not silently rewrite it. Propose edits explicitly.
- If PubMed tools are available, check at least one key claim against a primary source and cite the PMID.
