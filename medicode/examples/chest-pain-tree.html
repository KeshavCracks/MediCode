Chest pain triage

Source: ESC 2023 ACS guideline (illustrative)

Text version
  1. 12-lead ECG within 10 min
  2. STEMI pathway — activate cath lab
  3. High-sensitivity troponin 0/1 h
  4. NSTE-ACS management
  5. Consider alternative diagnoses
  6. 12-lead ECG within 10 min → STEMI pathway — activate cath lab (ST elevation)
  7. 12-lead ECG within 10 min → High-sensitivity troponin 0/1 h (No ST elevation)
  8. High-sensitivity troponin 0/1 h → NSTE-ACS management (Rise)
  9. High-sensitivity troponin 0/1 h → Consider alternative diagnoses (Rule-out)
