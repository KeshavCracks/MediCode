Adult sepsis — first hour

Emergency department, suspected infection

Source: Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 (illustrative)

Text version
  1. Suspected infection + qSOFA ≥ 2 or NEWS ≥ 5
  2. Blood cultures ×2 — before antibiotics if no delay
  3. Measure lactate
  4. Broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 h
  5. 30 mL/kg crystalloid if hypotensive or lactate ≥ 4 — volume from clinical_calc
  6. MAP < 65 after fluids → vasopressors; call ICU
